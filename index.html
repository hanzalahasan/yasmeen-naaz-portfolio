About

Empowering people through learning.

“Self-belief, continuous learning, emotional intelligence, financial awareness, and courage help women lead with confidence and compassion.”

Yasmeen Naaz is the Founder and Managing Director of QST Training and Consultancy in Qatar. With more than a decade of experience in the professional training sector, she leads accredited training solutions designed to empower individuals, companies, and communities.


Her leadership focuses on quality, strategic partnerships, operational excellence, internationally recognized qualifications, and meaningful community impact.
